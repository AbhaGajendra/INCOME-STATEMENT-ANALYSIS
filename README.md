## **📊 Income Statement Analysis Dashboard**

### **🎯 Project Objective**

An interactive and visually appealing financial analysis dashboard built to evaluate company performance across multiple dimensions. This project demonstrates skills in data visualization, financial modeling, and dashboard design, making complex financial data easy to interpret for stakeholders.

### **📖 Project Overview**

This project delivers a financial analysis dashboard that transforms income statement data into clear, actionable insights. 
It highlights key metrics such as revenue growth, expense trends, and profitability margins through interactive visualizations. Designed with a focus on data analysis, visualization, and financial storytelling, the dashboard demonstrates how technical skills can drive business‑ready insights and support data‑driven decision making.
________________________________________
### **🚀 Features**
- **Comparative Analysis:** Current vs. Previous period values with percentage changes.
- **Key Metrics:** Revenue, COGS, Gross Profit, Expenses, EBIT, Finance Costs, Net Income.
- **Visual Insights:** Trend charts and donut charts for Net Income Margin.
- **Interactive Filters:** Region, Year, and Month selectors for dynamic exploration.
- **Performance Indicators:** Highlights growth rates and margin changes.
________________________________________
### **🛠️ Tech Stack**
- **DAX:** Used for calculated columns and measures. 
- **Power BI:** Dashboard design and interactivity. 
- **Power Query:** For data transformation and cleaning.
________________________________________
### **📈 Insights from the Dashboard**
- **Strengths:**
-	Exceptional revenue growth **(+101%)**.
-	Net income margin remains strong at **14.43%**.
-	Finance costs are under control relative to revenue growth.
- **Weaknesses:**
-	COGS growth **(+113%)** outpaces revenue growth, squeezing gross margins.
-	Operating expenses **(+105%)** are rising faster than gross profit.
-	EBIT growth **(+74%)** lags revenue growth, showing efficiency challenges.
- **Opportunities:**
-	If the company can control costs, profitability could scale much faster.
-	Strong revenue growth indicates market demand is robust.
- **Risks:**
-	Continued expense growth could erode margins.
-	Rising COGS may indicate supply chain or procurement inefficiencies.
________________________________________
### **🔍 Key Strategic Insights**
1.	Profitability pressure is the biggest concern, not revenue.
2.	Rising finance costs and COGS are the major profitability drainers.
3.	Operational efficiency improvements and cost restructuring are critical.
4.	Revenue diversification and margin optimization should be the primary goals.
5.	Debt management and working capital optimization can significantly enhance bottom-line health.

### **🧭 Conclusion**
- The dashboard shows a company experiencing rapid expansion with strong revenue growth, but facing cost pressures that limit profitability gains. The key focus should be on cost management and operational efficiency to ensure that revenue growth translates into sustainable profit growth.
